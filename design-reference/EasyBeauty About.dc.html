Where it is made

One lab outside Lisbon, batch-tested before it leaves.

We produce in a single GMP-certified facility in Sintra. Every batch is tested for microbial load and active concentration, and the certificate of analysis is printed on the carton with the batch number.

Nothing is tested on animals, at any stage, in any market — including the ones that would require it. That decision costs us distribution and we are keeping it.
